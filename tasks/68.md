# Task 68: Clean Outcome-Based Posts UX — Hover-Lift Details, Compact Schedule, Delete Action & Badge Calming

## Goal
Execute user design feedback on `/posts`:
1. Change subtitle under "Posts" header from `"Nothing goes out until you approve it."` to `"Top three picks for you."`.
2. Remove the redundant inner section header (`Up next for review / The freshest 3 posts written for you...`).
3. Remove the loud `· 22` count from the `Needs you` Segmented filter tab.
4. Redesign Top 3 Curated cards to be pure poster cards by default; on hover, the card smoothly lifts up and reveals details (title, hook, channels) with:
   - Fixed, non-wrapping `[ Schedule · Thu 21:00 ]` button.
   - Sleek `[ Details ]` button (replacing the heavy "Studio" label).
   - Direct `[ Delete ]` action that permanently removes unwanted drafts via `DELETE /api/assets/[id]?mode=permanent`.
5. Apply smooth hover-lift and details reveal across the posts grid.
