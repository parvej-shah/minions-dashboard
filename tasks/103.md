# Task 103: Launch film "Write" (series film 1, Minions Post Studio)

## Goal
An energetic launch film (about 42 s, music and sound only) that shows the
busiest professionals what Post Studio does: it learns their work, writes
the post in their words with a cover, and they just say yes. Three cuts:
16:9 (1920×1080), 4:5 (1080×1350), 9:16 (1080×1920). Script: Film 1 in
../minions-marketing/post-studio-launch/SCRIPTS.md.

## Files to touch
- ../minions-marketing/post-studio-launch/film-write/ (HyperFrames source, outside this repo)
- tasks/103.md, tasks/status.md

## Constraints
- The post on screen is the real published post "Your 10-minute callback is
  racing the wrong competitor" from the bsse1610 workspace, under its real
  author (Parvej Shah, Founder of Minions.AI, with his own headshot), as the user chose.
  The database is read only; nothing is written or uploaded.
- Its cover is drawn by the app's real renderer from the post's saved cover_spec.
- UI shots rebuild only the pieces shown, with today's real labels
  ("Start with your LinkedIn profile.", "Read my profile", "Does this look
  like you?", the four progress lines, "Approve for Wed 9:00").
- Energetic: 120–128 BPM catalog music, the beat drops on the product name.
- Same end card family as "Covers", plus "Next: Covers".
- Nothing is posted anywhere; the files are handed to the user.
