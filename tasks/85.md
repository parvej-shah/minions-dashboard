# Task 85: Premium floating dock for the mobile bottom bar

## Goal
Replace the full-width phone tab bar (with its raised Posts tile) with a floating
glass dock: three equal tabs, one sliding pill that marks where you are, always-visible
labels, and calm tactile feedback. Supersedes the raised-center Posts button from task 84
(a raised center reads as a "create" action, but Posts is a place).

## Files to touch
- src/components/layout/TopNav.tsx (BottomTabs only)
- src/components/editorial/MobileActionBar.tsx (bottom offset only)
- src/components/ui/Toast.tsx (lifted offset only)
- src/test/mobileNavAndPwa.test.ts, src/test/mobileDock.test.ts
- tasks/85.md, tasks/status.md, .claude/memory.md

## Constraints
- Keep 3 tabs (Today · Posts · Brand) and the Posts "new post ready" dot.
- Every tab >= 48px tall; safe-area inset honoured; md+ unchanged (dock hidden).
- Indicator motion is disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
- No new dependencies; tokens from globals.css only.

## Acceptance tests
- [ ] Dock floats: rounded, inset from the screen edges, not a full-bleed border-t bar.
- [ ] One sliding indicator positioned from the active tab index; hidden when no tab is active.
- [ ] Tabs are equal: no raised/translated Posts tile.
- [ ] Tabs are >= 48px and have a visible focus ring.
- [ ] Indicator respects motion-reduce.
- [ ] MobileActionBar and lifted Toast sit above the dock (no 67px legacy offset).
- [ ] `npm test`, `npx tsc --noEmit` and lint pass.
