# Task 51: Retry a failed LinkedIn or Instagram post, and tell the user

## Goal
When a post went out but LinkedIn (or Instagram) refused it, the user can post it to that channel again from the Posted screen, and Today shows an alert for posts that did not go out everywhere.

## Files to touch
- src/services/linkedinPublish.ts (+test): posts a PUBLISHED draft to LinkedIn (carousel PDF or cover), records the failure or clears it
- src/services/retryPublish.ts (+test), src/app/api/assets/[id]/retry-publish/route.ts
- src/hooks/useEditorialDraft.ts, src/components/editorial/ReviewDecision.tsx, EditorialStudio.tsx: "Try LinkedIn again" button
- src/components/today/TodayView.tsx, src/lib/today.ts (+test): alert for failed posts

## Constraints
- Never posts twice: skips when the channel already has a post id. Facebook retry is out of scope (n8n only).
- Expired token: the message tells the user to reconnect in Settings.

## Acceptance tests
- [ ] `npm test`, `npx tsc --noEmit` and lint pass
- [ ] linkedinPublish: posts image or document, saves post id + time, clears its failure; records the failure on refusal; skips already posted / not connected
- [ ] failedPosts() lists published posts with a failure

## Do not touch
- n8n/, prisma/, .env*
