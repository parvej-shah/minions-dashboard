# Task 102: Launch film 1, "Covers" (Minions Post Studio)


## Goal
A 30–40 s Apple-style launch film for the built-in cover layouts of Minions
Post Studio, music only, in 16:9 (1920×1080) and 4:5 (1080×1350), ending on
minions.getminions.ai. Film 2 (bring your own design, task 103) opens where
this one ends.

## Files to touch
- ../minions-marketing/post-studio-launch/film-1-covers/ (HyperFrames project, outside this repo)
- tasks/102.md, tasks/status.md

## Constraints
- Every cover on screen is drawn by the app's real renderer (renderCoverPng)
  with the picker's demo author (Sam Rivera, Northwind Studio, Rex as photo).
  No client draft, no database read or write, nothing uploaded.
- UI shots rebuild only the cover card piece, with today's real labels
  ("Cover", "Layouts for this post", tile names, "Undo").
- Brand: Minions.AI logo files, Plus Jakarta Sans display; the covers keep
  their own fonts.
- Nothing is posted anywhere; the files are handed to the user.

## Acceptance tests
- [x] `npx hyperframes check --json` has no errors; warnings kept on purpose are named in REVIEW.md
- [x] snapshot review sheets read: every line readable at phone width, no jump at a cut
- [x] the film is 30–40 s, with music, in both sizes
- [x] the end card shows the Minions logo, "Post Studio" and minions.getminions.ai for at least 2 s

## Do not touch
- src/, prisma/, n8n/, .env*
