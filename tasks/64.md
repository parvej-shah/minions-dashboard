# Task 64: Ideas Page UI/UX Executive Overhaul (Audit Fixes)

## Goal
Transform `/ideas` from an administrative homework backlog into an executive autopilot for busy professionals:
1. Executive Scannability: Distill dense 50-word raw text paragraphs into punchy hooks and clean 1-line angles, eliminate redundant "Found by Minions" clutter, and remove prohibited eyebrow kickers above headings.
2. 1-Tap Agency ("Make next"): Allow users to promote any idea to #1 in the queue with a single click.
3. Inverted CTA Hierarchy: Make `Write now` a tactile primary button on the top idea, demoting `+ Add an idea` to a secondary action.
4. Frictionless Quick Capture: Streamline `AddIdeaDrawer` into a simple 1-line thought/link capture rather than asking for structured perspective essays.
5. Accessibility: Upgrade touch targets to $\ge$ 44px (`min-h-11`) and wrap titles in semantic `<h3>` headings.
6. Re-audit and verify health score improvement via `/impeccable` and `/intent`.
