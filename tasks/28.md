## Direction
- Welcome (Clio-like, approved as reference by the user): original line drawing
  (a person climbing a staircase of post cards toward a blue sun), headline aimed at
  the core ICP (B2B service founders who should post and don't):
  "You're good at what you do. LinkedIn just doesn't know it yet.", one line,
  one blue "Continue with Google", "Free for 14 days." (sign-in links, footer and Google logo trimmed).
- Setup: 4 thin progress segments + "n of 4"; big left-aligned title, one line, one
  white card, footer with a text link left and one blue pill right.
  1 Profile (PDF drop + website) · 2 Reading (rows tick as results arrive) ·
  3 Check it (one list, click a line to edit; proof as checkboxes) ·
  4 One story (pick from profile or "Something else" + optional detail) ·
  Writing (title, thin bar, one status line) · First post (preview, sign-off, Review my post).
- Removed: mascot speech bubbles, praise/feedback banners, story detail meter,
  number-key badges, % ring, step pills, scanning feed, story-kind selector
  (kind comes from the picked item; typed = LESSON), gold "ready!" headline, Find/Write/Publish tiles.
