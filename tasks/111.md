# Task 111: Google Analytics 4 (see what users do in the app)

## Goal
The app reports page views and four key actions to Google Analytics 4 (free), so the owner can see where users go and what they do. Nothing is sent unless `NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_ID` is set.

## Files to touch
- package.json (+ @next/third-parties)
- src/lib/analytics.ts (+ analytics.test.ts)
- src/app/layout.tsx
- src/hooks/useEditorialDraft.ts, src/components/today/TodayView.tsx, src/components/pwa/InstallPrompt.tsx (one `track()` call each)

## Constraints
- Page views come from the GA tag (route changes included). Custom events: `post_approved` (method: now | scheduled) and `app_installed`. No others (minimalism).
- No personal data: no email, name, post text, workspace or post ids in events.
- No-op when the tag is absent (local dev, tests, ad blockers); `track` never throws.
- The owner creates the GA4 property and adds NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_ID in Vercel; I do not touch .env* or deploy.

## Acceptance tests
- [ ] `npm test`, `npx tsc --noEmit` and lint pass
- [ ] track() calls gtag("event", name, params) when gtag exists
- [ ] track() does nothing, and does not throw, when window or gtag is missing or gtag throws
- [ ] layout renders the GA tag only when NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_ID is set
- [ ] post_approved fires after a successful approve-now, schedule, and Today "Approve for" (not on failure)

## Do not touch
- src/constants/nav.ts, docs/decisions/DECISIONS.md, .env*, prisma/, n8n/
