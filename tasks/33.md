# Task 33: Ten premium social poster designs

## Goal
Ten new cover designs that stand out in an Instagram and Facebook feed: image-led, big type, little text. Each one joins the cover rotation and the Studio's layout picker. The designs come from the 2026-11-10 Pinterest research and the reference pin (pin 1116048351441154976: grid paper, glow corners, a selected word, tilted tags).

## Files to touch
- src/lib/covers/social.tsx (new): the ten designs
- src/lib/covers/bannerTypes.ts, modern.tsx (export helpers, dispatch), layouts.tsx (pass tags)
- src/lib/covers/backgrounds.ts, posterType.ts (rotation)
- src/components/editorial/CoverImageCard.tsx (picker labels)
- src/lib/covers/social.test.ts (new)

## Constraints
- Brand colours only, our three fonts, sizes set by fit.ts. Copy that can't fit is rejected, never cut.
- No invented figures or counts. Tags come only from the post's own grid or list items.
- Satori: flex layout only.

## Acceptance tests
- [ ] `npm test`, `npx tsc --noEmit` and lint pass
- [ ] Every design draws a normal headline and throws CoverFitError on a long one (modern.test.ts covers every BANNER_DESIGN)
- [ ] TAGGED takes its tags only from short grid or list items (social.test.ts)
- [ ] Each design renders to a PNG, checked by eye on a contact sheet in two themes

## Do not touch
- n8n/, prisma/, .env*
