# Task 10: Modern banner designs as production cover types

## Goal
The 7 banner designs from task 9 (Torn, Select, Sticker, Notebook, Notes,
Post card, Circled) plus a new Cut-out design (the author's cut-out photo in
front of a giant word) are cover types next to the existing eight: picked
automatically in the rotation and offered in the Studio. The emphasis word
on these banners is set in a real italic (Instrument Serif Italic). A
workspace can upload a cut-out photo (transparent PNG) on the Brand page.
Then push to main (user OK, 2026-11-09).
