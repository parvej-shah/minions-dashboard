# Task 39: Instagram publishing channel

## Goal
A workspace can connect the Instagram professional account linked to its Facebook Page in one click ("Use my Facebook Page"). From then on, approved posts go to Instagram: the carousel when the post has one, otherwise the cover as a JPEG, with the Instagram caption.

## Files to touch
- src/types/channel.ts, src/constants/channels.ts, src/constants/postChannels.ts, src/lib/drafts/decision.ts
- src/services/channels.ts (+ connectInstagram), src/app/api/channels/instagram/route.ts
- src/services/instagramPublish.ts (+ test), src/app/api/internal/instagram/publish/route.ts
- prisma/schema.prisma + prisma/sql/2026-11-10-instagram-publish.sql (instagram_post_id, instagram_published_at)
- src/components/distribution/DistributionControlPanel.tsx (Instagram connect button), src/hooks/useDistributionChannels.ts
- src/components/editorial/EditorialStudio.tsx (Instagram tab shows when Instagram is on)
- n8n/workflows/03_review_publisher (Publish_to_Instagram after Merge_Publish_Results)

## Constraints
- The token is the Facebook Page token that is already vaulted. Copy it from vault to vault, and never return it to the browser.
- Instagram takes JPEG only, 2 to 10 carousel items, and a caption of at most 2200 characters, with no links.
- An Instagram failure never blocks the post: the blog, LinkedIn and Facebook still go out.
- Never publish twice: skip when instagram_post_id is already set.
