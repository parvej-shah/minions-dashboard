# Task 9: Poster-style carousel + modern banner samples (local only)

## Goal
Draw the Pinterest and blog findings (docs/research/carousel-design-2026-11-09.md) in code, as local samples the user can review before anything reaches the Studio:
- A **POSTER** carousel style:
  - one background that runs across the whole deck: a wavy ribbon with a node per page, and glows that sit on the seams so they cross pages
  - dark and light pages taking turns
  - highlighter boxes on key words
  - giant numerals
  - an asterisk mark beside the label
  - a split Contrast page
  - a faint giant word on Stakes pages
  - a round swipe button
- Two new slide layouts in every style:
  - **GRID**: 3 to 6 icon tiles
  - **PUNCH**: short lines, then checks
- Page 1 of a sample deck is the post's real stored cover.
- Experimental banner designs, as a separate module that is not offered to users yet:
  - torn-paper reveal
  - selection box
  - sticker tags
  - notebook sheet
  - notes card
  - tweet card
  - circled word
