# Task 11: Three premium carousel styles (local samples first)

## Goal
Next to EDITORIAL, BOLD, MINIMAL and POSTER, three more modern and premium
carousel styles, drawn in code and reviewed as local samples before the
writer is offered them:
- **SWISS**: light, type-led editorial. A faint column grid, hairline rules,
  the emphasis word in Instrument Serif Italic in the accent, a giant italic
  page folio at the bottom right, the closing page dark.
- **AURORA**: dark. Soft colour glows on one deck-wide drawing (they cross the
  page seams), a fine dot grid, the words on a frosted glass panel, a pill
  label, dot progress, a "Swipe" pill.
- **BENTO**: light. The page is a soft grey tray; the label, the words and the
  footer sit on raised paper cards; key words on a soft highlighter.
