# Task 32: Ask for the weekly update when a post is approved

## Goal
Right after a reviewer schedules a post, one quiet line asks "Anything new since last week?". The answer goes through the existing weekly-update flow (answer, notes, confirm) and the result says what it will do: the notes become post ideas. No new engine, no email (D16 stays).

## Files to touch
- src/lib/pulse/quickAsk.ts (+ quickAsk.test.ts)
- src/components/pulse/ApprovalUpdateAsk.tsx (new)
- src/components/editorial/ReviewDecision.tsx (optional `afterApprove` slot in the Scheduled view)
- src/components/editorial/EditorialStudio.tsx (shows it after a successful schedule)

## Constraints
- Reuse /api/pulse and /api/pulse/confirm; same privacy checks.
- A note the privacy check flags is not edited here; it sends the author to Today, where the full editor is.
- Shown once per approval; "Not now" hides it, never snoozes or skips the week.
