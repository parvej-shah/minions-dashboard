# Task 75: Quick Draft Top Picks Integration, Progress Banner & Dedicated Failed Posts Tab with Direct Retry

## Goal
Implement executive user feedback on `/posts`:
1. **Quick Draft Visibility & Progress**:
   - Provide immediate visual confirmation and an active progress banner when a user submits a Quick Draft via the balloon/drawer.
   - Integrate Quick Draft directly into the "Top Three Picks for You" section:
     - When a Quick Draft is generating, display an active, shimmering draft card right inside the Top Three Picks tray as `#1 Up next` with live progress ("Writing draft...", "Synthesizing thesis and visual cover...").
     - If the shelf has fewer than 3 posts, provide an elegant "+ Quick draft" card invitation in the Top Three Picks tray so users can trigger quick drafting directly from within their top picks.
     - When drafting completes, transition to a clear confirmation banner ("Post generated! “[title]” is ready for your review") with direct "Review draft" and "Schedule" actions, and the finished post occupies `#1 Up next` in Top Three Picks.
2. **Dedicated Failed Posts Tab**:
   - Add a 5th tab to the Posts library: `Failed` (with count badge if failed posts exist).
   - Posts with platform publish failures (`publish_failures` or `status === "FAILED"`) are moved to the `Failed` tab instead of hiding into the `Posted` tab.
   - On the `Failed` tab (and failed post cards), display clear error summaries and a direct, 1-tap **Retry** button (calling `/api/assets/[id]/retry-publish`) as well as Edit/Review and Delete actions.
