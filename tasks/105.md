# Task 105: Launch film "Yours" (series film 3, bring your own design)

## Goal
The loudest film of the series (music and sound only, about 36–44 s) that shows
"bring your own cover design": your brand already has a look, ask your designer
or your AI with our instructions, bring one HTML file, we check it, it takes your
colours and your face, pick it for any post, or use only yours. Three cuts:
16:9, 4:5, 9:16. Script: Film 3 in ../minions-marketing/post-studio-launch/SCRIPTS.md.

## Files to touch
- ../minions-marketing/post-studio-launch/film-yours/ (HyperFrames source, outside this repo)
- tasks/105.md, tasks/status.md

## Constraints
- Three invented sample brands (a calm dental clinic, a loud agency, an editorial
  law firm), as the user chose. Their designs are real contract HTML that passes
  the app's own `checkTemplate`, and every cover on screen is drawn by the app's
  renderer (Satori), never mocked.
- "Your face" and the feed use the bsse1610 workspace: Parvej Shah's real
  headlines, colours and cut-out. Database read only; nothing written or uploaded.
- UI copy is the app's: "Your own designs", "Get the instructions", "Give these to
  Claude, ChatGPT or your designer. They send back one HTML file.", "Copy the
  instructions", "Add your design", "Paste the HTML, or import the file.",
  "Check my design", "Check the preview", "This is your design with three
  different posts.", "<name> passed every check.", "Short post / Long post / Only
  the basics", "Layout", "Use only my designs", "Off: they alternate with ours.".
- A different style from "Write": editorial collage (paper, tape, hard cuts,
  stop-motion snaps), ~128 BPM catalog music that stops dead on "But is it yours?"
  and slams back in on the moodboard.
- Same end card family as the series.
- Nothing is posted anywhere; the files are handed to the user.

## Acceptance tests
- [x] the three brand designs pass `checkTemplate` (script prints ok for each)
- [x] `npx hyperframes check --json` has no errors in any of the three cuts; warnings kept on purpose are named in REVIEW.md
- [x] snapshots read: every line readable at phone width; in 9:16 key text sits between 250 px and 1500 px from the top
- [x] each cut is 34–46 s; the music is silent under "But is it yours?" and comes back on the moodboard (±0.1 s)
- [x] the end card shows the Minions logo, "Post Studio", "Try Post Studio" and minions.getminions.ai for at least 2 s
- [x] loudness about -14 to -16 LUFS, true peak ≤ -1 dBTP
