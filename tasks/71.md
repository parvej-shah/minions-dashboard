# Task 71: Mobile View Overhaul — Horizontal Snap Shelf, 44px Touch Targets & Reserve Touch Actions

## Goal
Overhaul the mobile experience on `/posts` using `/intent` and `/impeccable` design principles:
1. **Curated Top 3 Horizontal Snap Carousel on Mobile**:
   - Instead of 3 massive cards stacked in a 1700px vertical scroll, curated picks use a fluid horizontal snap shelf (`snap-x snap-mandatory flex overflow-x-auto gap-3.5 pb-2 -mx-4 px-4 sm:mx-0 sm:px-0 sm:grid sm:grid-cols-2 lg:grid-cols-3 sm:overflow-visible scrollbar-none`).
   - Cards fit comfortably within mobile viewports (`w-[85vw] max-w-[330px] shrink-0 snap-center sm:w-auto sm:max-w-none`).
   - Each card displays the poster, title, hook excerpt, and the two 44px touch-target CTA buttons (`Schedule · <slot>` and `Edit`) directly on mobile (no broken desktop hover emulation).
   - Discreet card counter/dots indicate the 3 picks.
2. **Accessible 44px Thumb Touch Targets**:
   - Primary `Schedule` and secondary `Edit` buttons provide minimum 44px height (`min-h-[44px]`) on mobile for effortless thumb operation.
   - Delete draft button provides an accessible 40x40px touch zone.
3. **Mobile-Specific Reserve Card Quick Actions**:
   - In the 2-column masonry grid on mobile, reserve cards show compact touch actions (`Schedule` and `Edit`) directly under the title.
4. **Copy & Control Polish**:
   - Draft reserve subtitle updates on mobile to omit desktop-only "Hover to preview".
   - Segmented filter control gains horizontal scroll protection (`overflow-x-auto scrollbar-none`) to prevent clipping on 360–390px screens.
