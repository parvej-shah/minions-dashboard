# Task 41: Picture pickers for cover layouts and carousel designs

## Goal
Choosing a cover layout or a carousel design works like Apple's Lock Screen or Photos filters: you see a picture of each choice, tap it, and the cover redraws. Undo takes you straight back. Layouts that can't be drawn are hidden, not greyed out. Instagram's and LinkedIn's Carousel / Single image choices are one segmented switch.

## Files to touch
- scripts/picker-thumbs.ts (new): draws public/images/pickers/*.webp from demo copy, with no DB and no upload
- src/lib/covers/pickerThumbs.ts (+ test): the sample paths, pickerLayouts (up to 4 "for this post", then the rest; unusable ones hidden)
- src/components/ui/ThumbPicker.tsx, src/components/ui/Segmented.tsx (+ test)
- src/components/editorial/CoverImageCard.tsx: layout strip under the cover, "All" to see every layout, Undo
- src/components/editorial/CarouselCard.tsx: design strip; LinkedIn Carousel / Cover image as a segmented switch
- src/components/editorial/social/SocialVariantTabs.tsx: Instagram Carousel / Single poster as a segmented switch

## Constraints
- Sample pictures are drawn once with demo copy (decided 2026-11-10), not per draft.
- Keep the radiogroup semantics: arrow keys move between choices and every tile has a name.
- Nothing new is written to the database; the same endpoints are used.
